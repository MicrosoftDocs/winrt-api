# Windows.Storage.Provider.StorageProviderSearchResult.MatchKind

<!--

## -description

Gets or sets the kind of match for the search result. This indicates why the entry is included, e.g. a **Lexical** or **Semantic** match.

## -property-value

The kind of match for the search result as a [StorageProviderSearchMatchKind](storageprovidersearchmatchkind.md) value.
