# Windows.Management.Update.WindowsSoftwareUpdate.ApproveCurrentAction(System.Boolean)

<!--
public Windows.Management.Update.WindowsSoftwareUpdateResult ApproveCurrentAction (bool approve);
-->


## -description

Approves or declines the action that the update is currently waiting on.

## -parameters

### -param approve

`true` to approve the current action; `false` to decline it.

## -returns

A [WindowsSoftwareUpdateResult](./windowssoftwareupdateresult.md) that indicates whether the operation succeeded.
