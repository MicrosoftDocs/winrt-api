<!--

## -description

Describes the outcome of the most recently completed action for a [WindowsSoftwareUpdate](./windowssoftwareupdate.md).

## -remarks

Read this from [WindowsSoftwareUpdate.ActionResultInfo](./windowssoftwareupdate_actionresultinfo.md) to determine whether the last action succeeded and, if not, why it failed.
